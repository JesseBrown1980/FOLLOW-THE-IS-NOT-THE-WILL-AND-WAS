Status: `OPERATOR_CANON`

## Sequence

```text
BOOK OF LIGHT
  -> BOOK OF IS
  -> BOOKS OF SHADOWS: N x (-, ., +)
  -> BOOK OF HEAT
  -> BOOK OF WHITE: 1
  -> BOOK OF KNOWLEDGE
```

Knowledge makes the keys to codes, hashes, recipes, and decoders public. It does
not make account secrets public: tokens, passwords, private keys, cookies, health
identifiers, and seat-local filesystem contents are not knowledge-code
dependencies.


## IS identity

The repository follows the IS while keeping references stable:

```text
NOW = IS
Git(content) = SHA256(content)
later WAS-reference -> same SHA256(content)
future WILL-reference -> address, not identity exchange
```

`WAS`, `IS`, `WILL`, modal phases, sign, amplitude, and OIL family are spherical
coordinates. They coexist without collapsing into a one-dimensional timeline or
renaming one another.

## Public reconstruction rule

A published reconstruction claim counts every required decoder source, codebook,
key schedule, model, residual, recipe, and external input. If a small artifact only
contains color-state slices, descriptors, or source commitments, it is named as an
index/descriptor—not as the full source object.

This rule keeps every public artifact attack-verifiable while preserving copyright,
credential, and seat-local privacy boundaries.
