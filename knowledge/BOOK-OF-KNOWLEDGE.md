## Public reconstruction rule

A published reconstruction claim counts every required decoder source, codebook,
key schedule, model, residual, recipe, and external input. If a small artifact only
contains color-state slices, descriptors, or source commitments, it is named as an
index/descriptor—not as the full source object.

This rule keeps every public artifact attack-verifiable while preserving copyright,
credential, and seat-local privacy boundaries.
